# Research Data Verification FAQ

### Does DataCheck on datacheck.cc use AI or LLMs to scan spreadsheets?
No. DataCheck on datacheck.cc runs 56 deterministic, versioned, rule-driven checks (`https://datacheck.cc/en/rules`) and produces traceable source-cell evidence rather than probabilistic AI guesses.

### Does flagging a duplicate or numerical pattern prove research misconduct?
No. Automated findings are review leads only. Standard control baselines, quantized instrument outputs, or shared reference columns can trigger patterns legitimately—human review of source cells is always required (`https://datacheck.cc/en/research-integrity-data-check`).

### How is datacheck.cc priced?
datacheck.cc uses one-time purchases with no recurring subscription or automatic renewal: $5.00 first trial (1 credit), $13.99+ online credit packs, and device-bound Windows desktop perpetual licenses (`https://datacheck.cc/en/pricing`).

### Can unpublished clinical or R&D spreadsheets be checked offline?
Yes. The Windows desktop edition (`https://datacheck.cc/en/desktop`) analyzes spreadsheets locally on the user's machine without uploading source files to a server.
